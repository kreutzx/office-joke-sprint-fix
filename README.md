# One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint — entry by @kreutzx

Entry for the make.fun bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-one-line-office-joke-24-hour-sprint-aaf86162

> Our CEO said 'we're a family' 14 times this quarter. My layoff email arrived Friday, subject line: 'A difficult family decision.'

## Why this line

The joke runs on a single contrast the whole office recognizes: "we're a family" is the warmest sentence in corporate language, and the company means it so literally that it fires people in the same vocabulary. The counted detail — said 14 times this quarter — makes the slogan sound measured and sincere right up to the turn, where the layoff email's own subject line quotes the family language back: a "difficult family decision." Nobody has to explain the hypocrisy; the email does it in the company's own words.

The program in `main.py` validates the line against the bounty's 200-character cap (it is 129) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
